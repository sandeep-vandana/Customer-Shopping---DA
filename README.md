# Customer_Shopping_Behaviour_Analysis
## Project Objective
The main objective of this project was to transform customer shopping data into meaningful business insights that can help businesses understand who their customers are, how they purchase, what products they prefer, and which factors influence their spending behaviour.

##Key Areas Analyzed:
Revenue generated by male vs. female customers
Customers who used discounts but still spent above average
Products with the highest average review ratings
Comparison of Standard vs. Express shipping based on purchase amount
Spending behaviour of subscribed vs. non-subscribed customers
Products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases
Customer segmentation into New, Returning, and Loyal customers
Most purchased products within each product category
Relationship between repeat purchases and subscription behaviour
Revenue contribution across different age groups

##Tools & Technologies:
SQL – Data analysis, aggregation, customer segmentation, and business queries
Power BI – Interactive dashboard and visualization
Excel/CSV – Dataset and initial data handling

##Business Value
The analysis provides a clearer understanding of customer purchasing patterns and helps identify high-value customer segments, popular products, discount behaviour, subscription patterns, and revenue-driving demographics.
This project demonstrates practical Data Analyst skills, including data exploration, SQL querying, business problem solving, KPI analysis, customer segmentation, and data visualization.
